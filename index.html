Session 1

Quick Reference

Session #: 1

Played On:

In-world Date: 7 Pollant 301 Bloom

PCs Present: Calanthe Orchidaceae, Dez, Kirill Amaterasu, Layla, Cleke

Recap

A read-aloud paragraph: what happened and what it meant. The party arrives separately in Benz during the Festival of Shooting Stars. A fallen star crashes into the village, releasing alien creatures that kill many inhabitants. The party unites and defeats the creatures. The The Thorns arrive shortly after to take statements from survivors, then secretly kill every witness. The party notices and escapes before they can be silenced. The Crown charges the party with Treason, Sedition, Arson, and other crimes and publicly attributes the destruction of Benz to a terrorist attack by the party. The falling star was not a natural phenomenon. It was a corrupted divine manifestation of an unnamed god, driven to Benz while being hunted by the Thorns. When the party destroyed the Star-Fallen creatures it released, the divine energy flooding the area activated inert fragments of the God of Second Chances each member happened to carry by coincidence. The Thorns arrived and killed every surviving witness. Every party member then shared the same vision: "Find the others... before she does." They fled Benz as fugitives, formally charged with sedition, arson, and treason.

  • The events unfold during Starsfall (7 Pollant). The Crown later used the incident to publicly frame the party: see Party Blamed for the Benz Incident.

Quick Reference

In-world Date: 1 Bloom

Location:

What Happened

In Year 1 of the current calendar, Queen Acantha Rosaceae consumed Veritas, the god of Truths and Records. The act granted her immortality. The calendar had been reset to Year 0 when she began her reign; Year 1 marked the year of the consumption. The consumption required locating The Index, the locus of Veritas's domain, where he could be engaged as a single incarnate thing rather than a diffuse presence. The battle was long and arduous. Veritas is the god of knowledge, not combat, and Acantha ultimately won by luck as much as preparation.

She knew that his Ledger records only what is true and that this property is tied to his own coherence: weaken him and the certainty of that truth weakens with him. While he faltered, she wrote a new entry in his Ledger: that Elara Aurum had survived The Grey Wilt. She believed this to be false. The contradiction, a lie forced into the instrument of absolute truth, shattered Veritas's coherence enough for Acantha to absorb his essence through the prepared artifact.

Nature of Gods in Elarosea

Developing Gods are real, present beings that embody a single domain and are incarnate where that domain runs purest; they do not, as a rule, tend their domains

Nature of Gods in Elarosea

Quick Reference

Category: cosmology

Era:

Related: Queen Acantha Rosaceae, Veritas, God of Second Chances, The Hunted God

Overview

Gods in this world are not ideas or distant powers. Each is a real being, the living body of a single domain, present wherever that domain runs and incarnate where it runs purest. A god of plenty exists while a district starves. A god of mending exists while fever empties a quarter and the carts go out full every morning. They are their domains. They do not, as a rule, tend them. This is not a flaw in the divine order by most reckonings. It is simply how gods are. To Acantha, watching people die for want of what a god possessed and spent nothing on, it looked like power held in a closed hand.

Details

A god cannot be consumed by force or surprise. A domain is too large to hold; reaching for it carelessly either scatters the loose essence back into the world or burns the one who grabbed for it to ash. To take a god whole, the consumer must find its locus: the single place where a domain runs so pure that the god is present there as one incarnate thing rather than a diffuse pressure in the air everywhere at once. Outside the locus there is no discrete thing to fight, let alone absorb. At the locus, the god is finite enough to be engaged directly.

Gods do not die, even when defeated and consumed. The divine essence persists inside whoever took it. Queen Acantha Rosaceae is the sharpest illustration of what a god is not. A true god is its domain and feels no strain in being so: it does not hold the domain, it is the domain. Acantha is flesh that holds divine essence the way a clay jar holds a forge-fire. The jar does not become fire. It is under strain every second it is not breaking. This is the line between a god and a being who has consumed one, and it is why a god in its landscape shows no strain while Acantha in hers accumulates it.

The Bloom

Developing Elarosea's year count, reckoned from the first flowering of the Rose Throne at Queen Acantha's accession

The Bloom

Quick Reference

Category: culture

Era:

Related: The Calendar, Queen Acantha Rosaceae, Consumption of Veritas, The Acanthine, Caelrosa, Veritas

Overview

Elarosea counts its years in Blooms. Each year of Queen Acantha Rosaceae's reign is one flowering of the Rose Throne, and the count runs from the throne's first flowering at her accession. A date is written with the year followed by Bloom: 12 Pollant 301 Bloom. Spoken, it is "the three hundred and first Bloom" or "year three hundred and one of the Bloom." Clerks in Caelrosa use the formal name Acanthine Bloom, which ties the count to its era, The Acanthine.

Year 0 Bloom is the accession, when the calendar reset. Year 1 Bloom is the year of the Consumption of Veritas. There is a quiet ugliness in the image fit for an immortal queen: the throne flowers once a year, every year, with no season in which it rests, and the count of those flowerings is now past three hundred. When Acantha took the Rose Throne, the court recorded that the throne itself came into flower, and the calendar was struck back to Year 0 to mark it. That flowering gave the count its name.

The trouble is that the records which would fix the date belonged to Veritas, god of truths and records, and she consumed him the next year. After Year 1 Bloom there was no longer any independent ledger against which to check Year 0. The reign's own beginning rests on the word of the woman who ate the only witness that could confirm it.
